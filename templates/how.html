How it works

The tool read {{ show.episodes }} episodes ({{ show.minutes|round|int }} minutes of story). An AI model judges the drama: which moments hook you and where to leave you hanging. Ordinary code then turns those picks into exact cut points and checks every rule: 20 to 60 seconds, a hook in the first 3 seconds, and a cut on the cliffhanger. A person approves each promo before it's posted.
